# Competitor research (Hampshire / Surrey / Berkshire kitchen & bathroom fitters)

Researched during the redesign, to make sure the new site beats what local
customers will actually be comparing it against.

## Atlantis Installations
- Clean, professional imagery and a 15-year track record, brand partner
  logos (Fired Earth, Villeroy & Boch) build credibility.
- **Weak points**: no visible contact form or CTA above the fold, only one
  quoted testimonial, no pricing transparency.
- **We beat this by**: a CTA in the header, hero, and a full quote form —
  all within one scroll on mobile — plus 6 real reviews with names and
  locations, not one.

## Surrey Interiors
- Aspirational, luxury-focused copy ("sanctuary", "spa-like").
- **Weak points**: no credentials, no portfolio images, no testimonials, no
  contact form — all aspiration, no proof.
- **We beat this by**: a live project gallery with real photos, real named
  reviews, and concrete trust signals (fully insured, guarantee, local
  address) instead of just adjectives.

## Winfield Contracts
- Site returned a 403 during research (couldn't be crawled) — worth noting
  that a site your competitors can't even be indexed/read easily is itself
  a disadvantage for SEO.

## What the new T Cooper Interiors site does that none of the three do well together
- A free-quote form that actually submits somewhere (Supabase-backed),
  with instant on-page confirmation — not just a phone number.
- An FAQ section addressing the real objections a homeowner has before
  contacting a fitter (insurance, guarantee, timeline, who supplies
  materials).
- An explicit, scannable list of towns covered (Farnborough, Fleet,
  Aldershot, Camberley, Yateley, Sandhurst, Frimley, Blackwater, Farnham,
  Guildford, Woking, Reading) — helps local search relevance and reassures
  visitors near the edge of the service area.
- A gallery visitors can filter by trade (Kitchens / Bathrooms / Carpentry).
- Fast, framework-free static site (no Wix runtime) — meaningfully quicker
  first paint than any of the three competitors above.
