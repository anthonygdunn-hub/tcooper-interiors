Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you start?

It depends on the size of the job and the season, but we'll always give you an honest start date at quote stage rather than an optimistic one we can't keep.

Are you insured?

Yes — we carry full public liability insurance, and are happy to share proof of cover before work begins.

Do you supply materials, or do I?

Either. We're happy to fit tiles, units or fittings you've already bought, or supply everything ourselves as part of the quote.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes — all workmanship is guaranteed. If something isn't right, we come back and put it right.

How do I get a price?

Fill in the quote form below or call us directly — we'll usually visit in person for kitchen and bathroom jobs so the price we give you is the price you pay.
